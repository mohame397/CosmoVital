## Source and asset acknowledgements

- NASA HRP, NASA standards and Canadian Space Agency resources: scientific/educational context, attributed at the point of use and in the local reference library.
- Lee et al. (2020): attributed published astronaut group numerical summaries; this is not a downloaded individual astronaut dataset or an approved medical reference range.
- Barger et al. (2014): four attributed ISS actigraphy sleep summaries for the 21-person ISS subgroup; no individual percentile distribution was reconstructed.
- BIDMC/PhysioNet recording: actual ground-hospital research, retained separately from astronaut and personal observations. See `dist/data/ATTRIBUTION.txt`.
- Earth image and self-hosted fonts: see `ASSETS.md` and the included asset license notices.
- esbuild: development build tooling. The runtime uses browser capabilities rather than a remote health-analysis service.
